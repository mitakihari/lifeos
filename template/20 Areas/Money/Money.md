# Money

**Purpose:** know where every dollar goes, stop surprises, build savings and credit, and afford the next big step.

## Standards (what "on track" means)
- Every transaction is in the ledger, imported at least weekly.
- Spending stays inside the monthly budget (`finance/budget.toml`).
- An emergency fund is building toward its target.
- The credit card is paid in full every month, so it never carries a balance.
- No surprise bills: everything recurring is listed below.
